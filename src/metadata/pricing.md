Seaside Talent Employer Tier Offerings
1. Free Access (Basic Employers)
Available to all employers at no cost.
Core Inclusions:
Free unlimited job postings – Reach a broad pool of talent.
Self-attested license information from applicants (type, number, state, expiration) – Basic compliance details provided directly by candidates.
Communication features: Message candidates directly within the app.
Ability to receive qualified candidate applications (credential filters apply based on self-attested data).
Understanding "Verified by Seaside" Candidates:
Candidates on Seaside Talent may display a "Verified by Seaside" badge on their profile, indicating that Seaside has conducted internal automated checks for licensure, OIG, SAM, and sex offender records.
Necessary: Employers on the Free Access tier cannot specifically filter for these "Verified by Seaside" candidates, nor do they receive any summary or direct access to the results of these checks. The badge serves as a signal of candidate proactiveness.
Optional Paid Add-Ons:
Seaside Credits:
100 Credits for $100
500 Credits for $425 (15% off)
1000 Credits for $800 (20% off)
Sponsored Listing: 3.33 Seaside Credits / day – Boost your job's visibility to top talent.
Employer Verification Badge: 10 Seaside Credits / month – Establish trust and enhance your company's appeal to candidates.
Campaigns: 50 Seaside Credits / each – Blast your open role to job seekers searching for their new position, filterable by area, licensure (self-attested), job title, and experience.
Limitations:
No access to the full candidate resume database.
No advanced resume search capabilities.
No integration with employer compliance tracking or onboarding workflows.

2. Subscription Employers (Resume Subscription) – $99/month
Intended for more proactive employers, recruiters, or smaller agencies who want to efficiently source pre-qualified talent.
Includes everything from the Free Tier, plus:
Access to our passive candidate pool via a searchable resume database.
Filter for and access candidates with the "Verified by Seaside" badge: Directly identify and connect with candidates for whom Seaside has performed automated internal licensure, OIG, SAM, and sex offender checks. This streamlines your initial screening process by focusing on a more reliable talent pool.
Note: Employers remain responsible for conducting their own thorough background checks and due diligence on credentials.
"Verified Employer Badge" (Included): Enhance your company's profile and build trust with candidates.
Filters by license type, state, setting preference, availability, and expiration (for all candidates, including those with "Verified by Seaside" status).
"Ping-to-apply" messaging workflow: Engage candidates directly (candidate must opt in before full profile access).
Profile metadata includes license tags, schedule, and location compatibility.
Use Case: Ideal for employers who need to source proactively and leverage Seaside's pre-screening capabilities to identify higher-quality, compliance-vetted candidates, without requiring full onboarding services.
Limitations:
Does not include comprehensive onboarding, document handling, automated license expiration alerts, or direct Tier 1 compliance tracking tools.
Does not provide direct access to the results of Seaside's candidate verification checks (summary results are reserved for Premium).

Our ultimate solution is for employers seeking maximum visibility, enhanced branding, and unparalleled access to the most thoroughly pre-vetted talent and compliance data.
Includes everything from the Subscription Tier, plus:
(3) Sponsored Roles Included: Your job listings are pinned to the top of search results and visually highlighted for maximum visibility.

Access is granted upon the employer's acceptance of a consent form acknowledging review and releasing Seaside from liability associated with this data.
Custom Employer Landing Page: Showcase your company culture and open roles on a dedicated, branded page.

Early Access: Be the first to try our newest features and enhancements.
