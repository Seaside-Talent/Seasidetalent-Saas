2. Our Mission, Vision, and Values
Mission: To make healthcare hiring seamless, ethical, and scalable by delivering recruitment and onboarding solutions that prioritize people, compliance, and long-term success.
Vision: To become the most trusted partner in healthcare hiring by removing barriers, reducing burnout, and raising the bar for recruitment and onboarding.
We envision a future where:
Healthcare teams grow faster because hiring is no longer a bottleneck
Candidates feel seen and supported, not ghosted or ignored
Compliance is not a burden, but built into the process
Technology powers decisions without replacing human connection
All team members are empowered to do meaningful, focused work, not get buried in chaos
Seaside Talent exists to build that future, one hire, one system, one relationship at a time.
Values:
People First, Always. We prioritize the wellbeing, dignity, and growth of every person we work with—whether employee, candidate, or client—because people are the foundation of great work.
Integrity Over Everything. We operate with honesty, transparency, and accountability, even when it is hard, because trust is earned and reputation is non-negotiable.
Compliance is Care. In healthcare staffing, cutting corners is not just risky, it is reckless. We hold the line on compliance to protect our clients, candidates, and communities.
Thoughtful Simplicity. We believe hiring should be clear, efficient, and human—not buried in red tape. We build processes that reduce friction and add value.

3. About Seaside Talent
Seaside Talent is a Massachusetts-based, remote-first recruitment and onboarding firm that serves healthcare organizations nationwide. Our team brings together expertise in human resources, healthcare staffing, compliance, and technology to support hiring operations with speed, precision, and trust.
Internally, we are committed to building a culture of integrity, transparency, and operational excellence. Our core service model—Recruitment-as-a-Service (RaaS)—offers clients a scalable and compliant hiring solution. As employees, you play a direct role in delivering this value by upholding our standards for process discipline, communication, and legal compliance.
While we provide onboarding and verification infrastructure for our clients, not all background checks or license verifications are conducted in-house. Self-attested candidate data is accepted at the application stage and verified downstream depending on the client’s service tier. Employees involved in screening or onboarding are expected to follow procedures outlined in Section VIII to ensure accurate handling of sensitive information.
We value collaboration, personal accountability, and ethical conduct in every aspect of our work. This handbook is designed to support your success in that mission.
